THE PROBLEM

Slide 7

THE PROBLEM

A demo that works is not a system you can trust

Generative AI is non-deterministic: same input, different output. Traditional pass/fail tests don't fit.

Quality is multi-dimensional — is it relevant? grounded? coherent? safe? — not a single boolean.

Failures are silent: a fluent, confident answer can still be wrong or unsafe.

You can't manage what you don't measure — and you can't ship what you can't defend.

“It works on

my examples”

is not evidence. Evaluation turns anecdotes into measurable, repeatable signal.

TAKEAWAY

Evaluation is how you replace “trust me, it works” with numbers your organisation can act on.

THE STAKES

What goes wrong without evaluation

Slide 8

THE STAKES

What goes wrong without evaluation

Hallucinations

Confident but false answers reach users and damage trust.

Safety incidents

Harmful, biased or unsafe content slips into production.

Silent regressions

A prompt or model change quietly breaks what used to work.

Compliance exposure

No evidence trail when the regulator, or the board, asks.

Each of these is measurable — and therefore preventable — with the right evaluators in place.

Evaluation is cheaper than an incident: you pay a little continuously instead of a lot suddenly.

For regulated and public-facing organisations, an evaluation trail is fast becoming a requirement, not a nicety.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Evaluation is how principles become practice

Slide 9

RESPONSIBLE AI

Evaluation is how principles become practice

Microsoft's Responsible AI principles — fairness, reliability & safety, privacy, inclusiveness, transparency, accountability — set the “what”.

Evaluation is the “how”: it turns each principle into a metric you can test and report.

Safety evaluators operationalise reliability & safety; groundedness supports transparency; the trace supports accountability.

For AI governance, evaluation is the evidence layer beneath your Responsible AI policy.

FOR AI GOVERNANCE

A Responsible AI policy without evaluation is a statement of intent. Evaluation makes it auditable.

THE PLATFORM

Evaluation is built into Microsoft Foundry

Slide 12

THE PLATFORM

Evaluation is built into Microsoft Foundry

Evaluators catalog — Microsoft-curated built-in evaluators + your custom ones, project-scoped with RBAC.

Two ways to run — the Foundry portal (no-code) and the azure-ai-evaluation SDK (code).

Local & cloud — run on your machine in the inner loop, or in the cloud at scale / in CI/CD.

Tied to observability — results land in your project and can flow to Application Insights.

Covers models, RAG apps and agents — one framework, many targets.

KEY CONCEPTS

The vocabulary of evaluation

Slide 13

KEY CONCEPTS

The vocabulary of evaluation

Evaluator

A function that scores an output on one dimension (e.g. relevance, groundedness, violence).

Evaluation data

query, response, context and (optionally) ground-truth — the inputs an evaluator reads.

AI-assisted vs NLP

LLM-as-judge for nuanced quality/safety, vs math metrics (F1, BLEU) for exact comparison.

Score & threshold

A number (e.g. 1–5 or pass rate) plus an acceptance bar you set (e.g. ≥85%).

Everything today is built from these four ideas — keep them in mind.
