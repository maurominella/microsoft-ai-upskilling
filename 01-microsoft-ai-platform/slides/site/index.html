The mental model

Three pillars carry the whole session — you need data to measure, and adversarial data to harden.

MEASUREEvaluators for quality, safety and agent behaviour — manually, in the cloud, or with your own custom evaluators.
GENERATESynthetic & simulated data so you always have meaningful cases to evaluate against.
HARDENAdversarially probe the system to surface safety and security risks before real users do.
