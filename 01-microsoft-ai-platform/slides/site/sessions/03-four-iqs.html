WORK IQ

Context on how your people work

Grounds agents in the flow of work across Microsoft 365 — mail, chats, meetings, docs.

Lets an agent understand roles, relationships and the way work actually happens.

Powers the interoperability of Agent 365 — agents that fit into human workflows.

Respects existing Microsoft 365 permissions — agents see only what the user may see.

FABRIC IQ

The semantic state of your business

Grounds agents in business entities and data — customers, campaigns, revenue — not raw tables.

Elevates data into the language of your business via ontologies / a semantic layer.

Built on Microsoft Fabric: analytical, real-time and operational data unified.

FABRIC IQ · IN PRACTICE

A “campaign” is a business entity, not a table

Slide 37

FABRIC IQ · IN PRACTICE

A “campaign” is a business entity, not a table

THE BUSINESS ASKS

Which campaign had the best ROAS last quarter, counting only active customers?

WITHOUT FABRIC IQ

The agent can only stop and ask back

There’s no ROAS column.

No “active customer” field exists.

Which join links Campaigns and Revenue?

How is “active” defined?

WITH FABRIC IQ

The agent answers in your business terms

BackToSchool_2024 — highest ROAS at 3.4

€128,000 attributed revenue from active customers

on €37,500 spend · +18% vs prior quarter

Best-performing channel: Paid Search

WHY IT WORKS

Fabric IQ already knows what a campaign, an active customer (≥2 purchases in 90 days), an attributed revenue (within 7 days of a click) and ROAS are — and which tables compose them. The agent doesn’t just read data; it understands the business.
