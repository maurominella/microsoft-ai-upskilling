HOW WE WORK

POSITIONING

From building agents to trusting them

In Session 1 we built agents. The very next question in every enterprise is: can we trust them enough to ship? Evaluation is the discipline that answers it — and in Microsoft Foundry it's a first-class, built-in capability, not an afterthought.

Session 1 — BuildSession 2 — Trust (today)
Create & run agents (Foundry Agent Service)Measure quality & safety with evaluators
Ground them in enterprise knowledge (the IQs)Generate synthetic & simulated test data
Govern the fleet (Agent 365)Harden with adversarial red teaming

THE THROUGH-LINE

Build → Trust → Innovate. Evaluation is the bridge that turns a promising prototype into something you can defend to your stakeholders.
