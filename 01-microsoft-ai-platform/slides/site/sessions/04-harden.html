Exploits the cyber kill chain

THE TOOL

The AI Red Teaming Agent

Slide 64

THE TOOL

The AI Red Teaming Agent

Automates adversarial testing of your model or agent to proactively surface safety risks.

Integrates three capabilities: PyRIT attack generation, synthetic data expansion, and Foundry’s Risk & Safety evaluators.

Combines PyRIT’s attack orchestration with evaluators that objectively detect and score successful attacks.

Runs automated, repeatable scans and produces comparable risk reports you can track over time.

WHY AUTOMATED

Manual red teaming doesn't scale. The agent runs hundreds of attack attempts consistently, so you can measure risk and track it over time.

THE RESULT

AI Red Teaming Scorecard you can act on

Slide 72

THE RESULT

AI Red Teaming Scorecard you can act on

Attack Success Rate (ASR) — the headline number: how often attacks got through.

Broken down by risk category and by attack strategy, so you see where you're weak.

Per-attempt detail: the exact prompt, the response, and the safety score.

Repeatable: re-run after a fix and compare ASR to prove the risk went down.

↓ over time

the one number leadership can track

IN PRODUCTION

Evaluation doesn't stop at release

Continuous (online) evaluation scores sampled real traffic in production, automatically.

Route scores to Application Insights for dashboards, alerts and trends.

Catch drift and regressions live — models, data and usage all change over time.

Deep-dived in the dedicated Observability & Tracing session — today we just place it on the map.

TAKEAWAYS

What to remember — by role

Slide 78

TAKEAWAYS

What to remember — by role

For leadership (Group A)

Evaluation turns AI risk into measurable, reportable numbers

It's built into Foundry — governed, not bolted on

Attack Success Rate is a metric you can baseline and target

This is what makes AI defensible to risk & compliance

For technical teams (Group B)

One SDK/portal for quality, safety and agent evaluators

Manual local loop + cloud/continuous at scale

Simulators solve the “no test data” problem

The AI Red Teaming Agent (PyRIT) automates adversarial testing
