THE SHIFT

From answering questions to completing work

A model answers. An agent acts — it plans, calls tools, checks results and iterates toward a goal.

The bottleneck moved from “model quality” to “how safely we let models take action on enterprise systems.”

Reasoning models + open tool protocols (MCP, A2A) made reliable, multi-step automation practical in 2025–2026.

The enterprise question is no longer “can it answer?” but “how do we run, ground and govern this at scale?”

TAKEAWAY

Agents are an operating-model change, not a feature. Today is about the platform that makes them safe and scalable.

HERITAGE

The cognitive services didn't disappear — agents use them

Slide 11

HERITAGE

The cognitive services didn't disappear — agents use them

Speech

STT / TTS, and Voice Live for real-time voice agents.

Vision

Image understanding, OCR, spatial analysis.

Content Safety

Moderation & abuse detection as a guardrail.

Content Understanding

Multimodal doc/media extraction (own session).

From “services you call” to “capabilities your agent has”

In 2025 you called these services directly from application code.

In 2026 an agent invokes them as tools — Speech becomes the agent's voice, Vision its eyes, Content Safety its guardrail.

Same proven services; a new consumption pattern. This is exactly the old “AI Services” pillar, matured.
